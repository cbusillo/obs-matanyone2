## What obs-matanyone2 does

obs-matanyone2 is an OBS filter that aims to remove the background while retaining a person and props such as a chair, microphone, and boom arm.
It runs MatAnyone 2 video matting through Core ML on Apple Silicon; stable segmentation in the Director's real scene is not established by this description.
Evidence: [README.md](README.md) and the existing repository guide.
